The Basenji Sweater

One piece, knitted from the collar down, in the round, with no seams. A folded rib collar, a shaped chest, two proper leg openings, a waist that follows the tuck-up, and a stretchy ribbed hem. Every number below is calculated from your dog and your gauge — change any input and the whole pattern rewrites itself.

Before you cast on

Three things that go wrong, and how not to do them 1 · Twisting the join. Unfixable. Lay the cast on flat and check the whole circle before you join — see lesson 7.
2 · A tight cast on or bind off. The collar has to go over the head and the hem over the ribcage. Both edges must stretch.
3 · Not swatching. If your gauge is not what you typed above, none of these numbers apply. It is twenty minutes against three evenings.
