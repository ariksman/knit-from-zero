Why this site exists

Most knitting tutorials skip the part where you don't know anything

They say “cast on 80 stitches” without saying what a stitch is. They say “work in stockinette until the piece measures 20 cm” without saying which way to hold the needles. And they hand you a pattern sized for a dog that is not your dog.

This course does it the other way round. Lesson 1 is what is actually happening when you knit — the mechanics of one loop pulled through another. By lesson 10 you can read any pattern in the world. In between, you make a real thing.

Everything is generated, not photographed

Every diagram here is built in your browser from real stitch geometry — flat as SVG, and in three dimensions as WebGL, because some things about knitting cannot be drawn flat.

The maths is done for you

Type in your dog's neck, chest and back length plus your own gauge. The pattern generator rewrites every stitch count, every round number and the yarn quantity.

The project

The Basenji Sweater

One piece, knitted from the collar down, no seams. It has a folded rib collar, proper leg openings, a shaped waist that follows the breed's tuck-up, and a stretchy ribbed hem. It is designed for a dog of about 10 kg — and then resized to your dog by the generator.
