Rules of thumb worth memorising

SituationRule
Long-tail cast on, DK yarnAbout 2.5 cm of tail per stitch, plus 20 cm spare.
Picking up along a vertical edge3 stitches for every 4 rows.
Picking up along a horizontal edge1 stitch for every stitch.
Ribbing for a snug edgeWork it on needles one size smaller than the body.
2×2 ribStitch count must be a multiple of 4.
1×1 ribStitch count must be a multiple of 2. Grips harder than 2×2 on small tubes.
Gauge is too tight (too many sts per 10 cm)Go up a needle size.
Gauge is too looseGo down a needle size.
Wool that will be machine washedMust say “superwash” or “machine washable” on the band. There is no way to make untreated wool safe afterwards.
Buying yarnOne ball more than the estimate, all from the same dye lot.
